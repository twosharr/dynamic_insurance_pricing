# Executive Summary & Business Case: UC070 AI-Powered Dynamic Pricing for Insurance

## 1. Project Background
Personal auto insurance is undergoing the most radical underwriting shift in its 125-year history. For decades, carriers relied on static demographic variables (age, vehicle age, residential zip code, credit tier) as crude proxies for risk. 

This model introduces severe market dysfunction:
1. **Inequity:** A cautious 35-year-old nurse driving 5,000 miles a year without ever touching their phone pays nearly the same premium as an aggressive commuter in the same zip code who texts at 75 mph.
2. **Adverse Selection:** Tech-savvy safe drivers are actively fleeing legacy carriers to join telematics-native insurers (Root, Lemonade, Progressive Snapshot, Tesla Insurance), leaving traditional books of business concentrated with unpriced risk.
3. **Regulatory Pressure:** State insurance commissioners are progressively banning credit score and territory-based rating due to disparate socioeconomic impact.

**UC070** solves this by establishing an end-to-end **AI-Powered Usage-Based Insurance (UBI) & Dynamic Pricing Engine**, leveraging smartphone/OBD sensor streams to reward safe driving in real time.

## 3. Financial Model & Return on Investment (ROI)

- **Baseline Traditional Book:**
  - Written Premium: \$14,500,000 (\$1,450 avg)
  - Incurred Losses (68.4% Loss Ratio): \$9,918,000

  - Operating & Telematics Technology Costs (25.5%): \$3,386,400

  - **Net Underwriting Profit Lift:** **+\$2,029,920 per 10,000 policies (+250% profit expansion)**

## 4. Key Capabilities Demonstrated in Platform
1. **Interactive Pricing Simulator:** Adjust hard braking, rapid acceleration, speeding, phone distraction, night driving, and mileage sliders with instant dollar-for-dollar updates.
2. **Leaflet GPS Route Hazard Visualizer:** Plays back real commuter drives with sensor event pins (hard braking, speeding, phone unlocks).
3. **Explainable AI (XAI) Waterfall:** Itemizes exactly why a driver pays what they pay, eliminating black-box opacity.
4. **Live Weather Hazard API (Open-Meteo):** Free real-time integration dynamically adjusting rates for rain, snow, and freezing road conditions.
5. **Actuarial & Credibility Rigor:** Implements Bühlmann-Straub credibility weighting to protect consumers against premature penalties.
